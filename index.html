About SNIA

The SLASSCOM National Ingenuity Awards is Sri Lanka’s leading platform recognizing technological innovation across corporates, startups, universities, and schools.

Now in its 8th edition, SNIA highlights groundbreaking solutions that demonstrate originality, commercial potential, and measurable societal impact.

Mission

To inspire innovation, build intellectual property, and support Sri Lanka’s ambition of becoming a US$5 billion knowledge economy by 2030.

Eligibility

Open to registered companies in Sri Lanka, ICT startups registered after 1 Jan 2022, University students, and School students.

Judging Criteria

Submissions are rigorously evaluated based on Originality, Scalability, Practicality, and Impact.

Evaluation Process

A structured journey:
Stage 1 – Initial Screening,
Stage 2 – Provincial Evaluation,
Stage 3 – National Finals.

Our Focus

What SNIA Does

Driving Sri Lanka's transition from service-based to product innovation.

Discover & Recognise

Identify regional innovations...

Identify innovations from schools, universities, startups, and enterprises across Sri Lanka, ensuring no great idea goes unnoticed.

Evaluate & Validate

A multi-stage assessment...

Entries are assessed through a rigorous multi-stage process: Initial screening → Provincial review → National finals by industry experts.

Celebrate & Amplify

Rewarding ICT excellence...

Winners are celebrated at a grand ceremony at ITC Ratnadipa, Colombo, giving them national recognition and media exposure.

Inspire the Next Wave

Empowering young minds...

Encouraging the next generation of innovators and entrepreneurs through school and university category highlights.

Build IP Ownership

Securing Sri Lanka's future...

Promoting Sri Lanka's transition from a service-based economy to a product-based one by fostering local intellectual property.

Attract Investment

Connecting to capital...

Highlighting high-potential innovations to local and global investors, accelerating startup growth and scaling.
